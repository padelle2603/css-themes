# Legal Assessment

> **Important:** This document is an **informational summary**, not legal advice. It does not create an attorney-client relationship. Legal assessments depend on specific facts and jurisdiction. For absolute certainty, consult a qualified lawyer licensed in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

This assessment concerns the distribution of two original CSS theme files:

- A dark theme for the YouTube Music web interface (`pear-music-theme/dark.theme.css`), applied via a browser user-style extension on the user's own device.
- CSS themes for Discord client modifications (`Vencord/*.css`), applied by the user on their own device.

Both files are original works authored for this repository. They contain no third-party copyrighted assets, no proprietary source code from Google/YouTube or Discord, and no code intended to circumvent access controls or monetization.

## International law (Berne Convention / WIPO / DMCA)

- **Copyright:** The CSS is an original work of authorship. Applying CSS on one's own device is a form of UI personalization and does not reproduce, distribute, or publicly communicate protected content of the target services. No infringement under the Berne Convention or the U.S. DMCA arises from the themes themselves.
- **Technological Protection Measures:** The themes do not circumvent technological protection measures, so anti-circumvention provisions (e.g., DMCA § 1201) are not implicated.
- **Trademarks:** The themes do not use the logos or trade dress of third parties in a way that suggests affiliation or endorsement.

## European Union law

- **InfoSoc Directive 2001/29/EC:** No reproduction or communication to the public of protected works occurs. Article 6 (protection of technological measures) is not relevant: cosmetic styling does not defeat access or copy controls.
- **DSM Directive 2019/790:** Not applicable; no text-and-data-mining or online content-sharing issues arise.
- **GDPR (EU) 2016/679:** Not applicable to the author. A static repository of CSS processes no personal data and exposes no personal information.
- **e-Commerce Directive 2000/31/EC:** The repository contains lawful content. Even if a user misused the files, the author, as a mere host/provider of content, would not be responsible for the acts of users.
- **Consumer law (Directive 2011/83/EU):** Not applicable; this is a free, non-commercial demonstrative project.

## Italian law

- **Legge 633/1941 (copyright law):** The themes are original works; there is no reproduction of protected content and therefore no infringement.
- **Civil Code, art. 2043 (tort liability):** Liability requires wrongful conduct, damage, and causation. Publishing demonstrative CSS themes is not wrongful conduct. Any consequence (e.g., account bans) arises from the user's own decision to use client modifications, breaking the causal chain to the author.
- **Civil Code, art. 2050 (dangerous activities):** Not applicable; hosting static stylesheets is not a dangerous activity.
- **Criminal law:** No relevant criminal provision is engaged, as the files contain no malicious code and do not circumvent technological protections.
- **Contractual (ToS) effects:** Violations of Discord's or Google's Terms of Service produce effects only between the service and the user, never against a third-party author who has no contractual relationship with those services regarding the user's conduct.

## Conclusion

Provided the repository (i) contains only original content, (ii) does not circumvent technological protection measures, (iii) is clearly labeled as demonstrative, and (iv) does not encourage or facilitate misuse, its publication is lawful under international, EU, and Italian law.
